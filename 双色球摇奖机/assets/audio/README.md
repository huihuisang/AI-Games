# Machine Audio Sources

All audio is served from this directory. Playback does not contact third-party services.

## Collision and rolling samples

- Author: Kenney Vleugels / Kenney
- Pack: Casino Audio 1.1
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- Original files: `chips-collide-1.ogg` through `chips-collide-4.ogg`, `chip-lay-1.ogg`, and `dice-shake-1.ogg` through `dice-shake-3.ogg`
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal. See `KENNEY-LICENSE.txt` and https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Delivery edits: mono PCM WAV, 24 kHz, high-pass at 160 Hz, low-pass at 7.5 kHz, short edge fades.

## Motor recording

- Author: supersnd
- Title: Old computer motor
- Source: https://freesound.org/people/supersnd/sounds/331426/
- Download: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/331/331426_3877322-hq.mp3
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal, as stated on the source page: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Delivery edits: use seconds 2–8, mono PCM WAV at 24 kHz, high-pass at 65 Hz, low-pass at 1.7 kHz, 200 ms overlap for a continuous loop, adjusted level.

These recordings are material for a designed sound effect. They are not a field recording of an actual lottery machine.
